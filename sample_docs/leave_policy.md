# Nimbus Labs – Leave Policy (2026)

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets **24 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, credited monthly (2 days per month). Up to **10 unused days** can be carried forward to the next year; anything above 10 lapses on 31 December. Leave must be applied for in the HR portal at least **5 working days** in advance for absences longer than 2 days.

## Sick leave
Employees get **12 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave does not carry forward. For sick leave longer than **3 consecutive days**, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within a week of returning.

## Parental leave
- Birthing parents: **26 weeks** of fully paid leave.
- Non-birthing parents: **4 weeks** of fully paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.
- Adoptive parents of a child under 1 year receive the same entitlement as birthing parents.

## Public holidays
The company observes **12 public holidays** per year. The list is published on the intranet every December. If you work on a public holiday at your manager's request, you get one compensatory day off, to be used within 60 days.

## Work from home
Employees may work from home up to **3 days per week** with manager approval. Tuesdays and Thursdays are team-anchor days when everyone is expected in the office.
