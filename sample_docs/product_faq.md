# Nimbus Cloud Backup – Product FAQ

## What is Nimbus Cloud Backup?
Nimbus Cloud Backup automatically backs up laptops and servers to encrypted cloud storage. Files are encrypted with **AES-256** on the device before upload, and the encryption key never leaves the customer's device unless they turn on key escrow.

Annual billing gives **2 months free**. All plans include a **30-day free trial**; no card is needed for the trial.

## How long are deleted files kept?
Deleted files and old versions are kept for **30 days** on Starter and Family, and **1 year** on Business.

## How do I restore files?
Open the Nimbus app, go to **Restore**, pick the device and the date, then choose the files. Restores of more than 500 GB can be shipped on an encrypted drive (Business plan only, ₹4,999 per drive).

## Which systems are supported?
Windows 10 and later, macOS 13 and later, and Ubuntu 22.04 and later. Mobile apps (Android and iOS) can browse and download backups but can't back up phones.

## Support
Starter and Family plans get email support with a 24-hour response time. Business plans get 24×7 phone and chat support with a **1-hour** response target for critical issues.
