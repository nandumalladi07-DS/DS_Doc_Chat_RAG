# Nimbus Labs – Travel & Expense Policy

## General rules
All expenses must be submitted in the Expensify app within **30 days** of being incurred, with an itemised receipt for anything above **₹500**. Expenses older than 90 days will not be reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Travel
- Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for longer flights.
- Trains: AC 2-tier or equivalent.
- Hotels: up to **₹6,000 per night** in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) and **₹4,000 per night** elsewhere.
- Local cabs to and from airports and client sites are fully reimbursed.

## Meals
Daily meal allowance while travelling is **₹1,500** in metro cities and **₹1,000** elsewhere. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Equipment and learning
- Home-office setup: one-time allowance of **₹15,000** for a chair, desk or monitor, available after completing probation.
- Learning budget: **₹40,000 per year** for courses, books and conference tickets. Courses above ₹10,000 need prior manager approval.

## Approvals
Expenses up to ₹25,000 are approved by your manager. Anything above ₹25,000 also needs approval from the department head.
